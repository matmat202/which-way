Which Way Next?

Ten years of a US economic number, up to a month in the past. A year later, was it higher or lower? Ten a day, from landslides to near-flat years.

Round 1 of 10

      Data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — 14 monthly US series (unemployment, interest rates, inflation, oil, housing, exchange rates, sentiment, saving), each one that a fixed rule like "always higher" or "same way as last year" gets right no more than about two times in three. Weekly series are monthly averages. A new ten every day at your midnight; everyone gets the same ten. Add ?day=2026-11-02 to play an earlier day. Nothing leaves your browser.
